The chaos serpent Apep (Apophis) attempts each night to swallow the sun god Ra; if it ever succeeds, the world ends

The entire cosmos, gods included, is consumed in a great conflagration, then everything is reborn from the ashes.

A final battle purges evil; a river of molten metal cleanses humanity, and the world is restored to its original perfect state.
